METHOD (reproducible)

Part 1 - techi_articles.csv
Source: live pages at https://www.techi.com/category/<slug>/ (page 1 only),
fetched 2026-08-31. Slugs used: ai-intelligence, markets-equities,
crypto-defi, tech-breakthroughs, policy-impact, research-tools-guides
(these are the real slugs behind the site's "AI", "Markets", "Crypto",
"Breakthroughs", "Policy", "Guides" nav labels). For each section, the 20
most recent article cards on page 1 were taken in the order shown (site
already sorts newest-first); one non-article "live market board" widget on
the Crypto page was skipped. Fields taken directly from the page: url,
section, headline, author, date_shown (verbatim). No login, no pagination
past page 1, single sequential fetches (well under 1 req/2s).

Three fields are NOT on the site and are computed, not scraped:
- headline_word_count: whitespace split of the headline string.
- contains_a_number: regex \d anywhere in the headline.
- names_a_company: headline contains a whole-word match against a fixed
  ~65-name list of companies/tickers seen in this sample (Nvidia, Tesla,
  Google, etc.) - a judgment call, not a site field.
- format: Guides section -> "Guide" always. Other sections -> "Explainer"
  if the headline matches a definitional pattern (e.g. starts "What Is",
  contains "Field Guide"/"Checklist"); "Analysis" if it contains an
  interpretive marker (e.g. "isn't", "the real", "test", "catch", or
  starts "Why "); else "News". Exact patterns are in build_csv.py. This is
  the analyst's classification, not a TECHi field - treat as a judgment
  call, not ground truth.

14 of the 120 sampled urls appear twice (cross-listed on two category
pages) - a real site behavior, not a scraping error.

Part 2 - traffic_data_invented.csv
The 12-month sessions/signup table is the invented data supplied in the
brief, transcribed as given and kept in its own file/sheet, never merged
with Part 1.

Reproduce: python3 build_csv.py && python3 traffic_analysis.py &&
python3 analysis.py (pandas, matplotlib, openpyxl; no other dependencies).
